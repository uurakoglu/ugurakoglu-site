Thesis

M.Sc. thesis · Ege University · September 2025 · Supervisor: Assist. Prof. Dr. Coşkun Harmanşah

PLM Migration Strategies and Approaches from Windows to Open Source Servers

Many companies run their PLM systems on Windows servers and pay for it in licences and limited flexibility. My thesis asks what it takes to move such a system to Linux, looking at the technical, organisational and financial sides together.

Its centre is a case study from a 500-user PLM environment: an ENOVIA upgrade from R2021x to R2024x in which the application and document servers moved from Windows to Oracle Linux. The database stayed on Windows, on the same hardware.

  • In the three months after the switch, help-desk requests about a slow system fell to a quarter of their earlier level.
  • Windows Server licences dropped from nine machines to two. Together with a disk upgrade that was no longer needed, direct savings passed 15,000 USD in the first year.
  • The migration is a change-management project as much as a technical one. Training, management support and clear roles decided how smoothly it went.
